About Me

Hello, i'm Sebastian Day, i'm a PhD student at University of Edinburgh supervised by Oisin Mac Aodha and Hakan Bilen. I work on Machine Perception and controlled conditioning of generative models.

I obtained my undergraduate and MSci degrees in Theoretical Physics from the University of Cambridge, specialising in Quantum Field Theories and General Relativity. My research project was supervised by Eugene Terentjev. Prior to working in Machine Learning I was an investigative journalist specialised in Quantitative Finance.
